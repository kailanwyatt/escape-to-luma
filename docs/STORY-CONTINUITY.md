# Campaign continuity pass

The canonical story remains: a probe captures Spark, containment fails, Spark follows an unknown signal, the Nebula proves to be a relay, The Key reveals Luma, and reunion leads to free exploration.

## Connected locations

World arrival text now connects rooftop weather towers, an orbital transfer facility, a satellite graveyard, a lunar relay, distant Jump Gates, the Nebula and the ancient network. Pressure shutters and sliding doors belong to the transfer facility; reflectors belong to its satellite equipment. Jump Gates explain the later large changes in distance.

## Milestones

Each world's successful finale now presents its own story before the existing results/reward flow: Lockdown Broken, Skybreak, The Storm, Escape Velocity, Orbital Graveyard, Far Side, Collision Course, False Home, The Key, and reunion. The final three have distinct lightweight still illustrations in the existing React Native story screen. These are narrative screens, not completed real-time 3D cinematic scenes.

Acknowledgements use separate stable departure IDs in the existing save. Story screens do not grant rewards. Entering the first level of the next world recovers an unread preceding exit only if its finale is recorded as cleared, then presents the arrival. Returning to a completed Level 150 can recover the unread reunion. Acknowledged stories do not repeat; old rewards/progression are preserved.

The map keeps its destination unknown until Homeward unlocks through completion of The Key. Network signal strength remains strong rather than located; locked worlds show unknown signals. Legacy opening copy no longer names Luma as the signal source. Branding may still name Luma.

## Remaining art work

Sky, lunar and later worlds still need distinct environment kits. Major milestone cinematics, the playable multi-stage finale, a real-time reunion scene and final music resolution remain production work. This pass completes the narrative-screen continuity, not those 3D assets.
