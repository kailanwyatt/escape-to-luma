# World Progression (as implemented)

| World | Levels | Status | Primary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Containment | 1–15 | **Playable** | Rotor → Lockdown finale |
| 2 The City | 16–30 | **Playable** | Sliding gate + wind |
| 3 The Sky | 31–45 | **Playable** | Moving rings + crosswind |
| 4 Upper Atmosphere | 46–60 | **Playable** | Iris + gravity reduction |
| 5 Orbit | 61–75 | **Playable** | Pendulum + low gravity |
| 6 The Moon | 76–90 | **Playable** | Orbiter + gravity wells |
| 7 Asteroid Belt | 91–105 | **Playable** | Drifting blockers |
| 8 The Nebula | 106–120 | **Playable** | Phase fields |
| 9 Ancient Network | 121–135 | **Playable** | Shifting apertures |
| 10 Homeward | 136–150 | **Playable** | Combined hazards → HOME |

All 150 core levels are authored and registered. Worlds are unstubbed on the Journey map.

Journey destination shows **UNKNOWN** until network/homeward unlock; **HOME** after campaign complete.

World clear unlocks the next world + completion Spark + world shards.  
Level 150 sets `campaignCompleted` and Endless Voyage.

Home signal / distance strings live on each `WorldDefinition`.
